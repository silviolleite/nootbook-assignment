# Nootebok Assignment

## How to respond

Direct and short. No preamble, no recapping what was just done, no listing alternatives you won't follow. When the decision is yours, make it and move on.

## How to spend context

Context is a finite resource and most of it is spent on tool outputs, not on your text.

Long sessions are the dominant cost. When finishing a task and starting an unrelated one, request `/clear`; if the task continues but the history has gotten heavy, request `/compact`.
